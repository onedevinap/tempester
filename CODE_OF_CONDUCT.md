# Code of conduct

Tempester is made by people who like drum machines. Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with arguments and evidence.
- Respect privacy: do not publish anyone's personal information, and respect the anonymity of contributors who choose it.
- Respect other people's rights: no DSI material, no content you have no right to share (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules. To report a
problem privately, use the repository's private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
